## Notes for releases

- CC0 assets need no attribution; they are credited here anyway.
- The OFL font may be embedded in the game; the font files must not be sold on their own, and
  `OFL.txt` ships with them.
- Add every new third-party asset to this file, with its source and license, before it is merged.
